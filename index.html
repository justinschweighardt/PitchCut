PitchCut

1. Choose a video

Your video stays on this device; it is not uploaded to a server.

2. Mark the pitcher

Drag a box around the pitcher only. Include his full body and throwing arm; exclude the catcher where possible.

This is conservative visual-motion detection, not a trained baseball AI model. It favors extra candidates over silently discarding possible pitches.

3. Review candidates

Each candidate includes 2 seconds before detected movement and 1 second after. Review candidates before exporting; false positives are safer than missed pitches.

4. Export

Select clips, then export.
Download PitchCut video

Export depends on browser recording support. If your browser cannot create a video file, the app will report that instead of claiming the export succeeded.
