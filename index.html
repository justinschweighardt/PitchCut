What the AI does—and does not do

PitchCut loads Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, a machine-learning model that estimates body landmarks. PitchCut then applies rule-based movement thresholds to identify possible pitching sequences. Pixel-motion detection runs as a backup.

This is AI-assisted candidate detection, not a baseball-specific model trained on labeled pitching footage. It can miss pitches or flag other movements. Review the candidates before relying on them.

Video processing runs in your browser. The MediaPipe code and model are downloaded from Google-hosted public files when the scan starts. Keep Safari open and prevent the phone from sleeping during analysis.
