What the AI does — and does not do

PitchCut uses Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, a machine-learning model that estimates 33 body landmarks. The pitching-sequence score is a rule-based analysis of those landmarks, not a baseball-specific model trained on labeled pitches.

This is AI-assisted candidate detection, not guaranteed pitch recognition. It can miss pitches when Brayden is very small, occluded, or poorly lit. It can also flag non-pitch movement. The motion backup and manual review are included to reduce missed pitches.

The video itself is processed on your device. MediaPipe’s library/model are downloaded from Google’s public hosting when you run the scan. MediaPipe documentation notes that video input is processed on-device; its library may send usage metrics.
